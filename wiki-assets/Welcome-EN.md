# YT Looper: welcome

[Español](/AdriRRP/yt-looper/wiki/Inicio-ES)

YT Looper repeats an exact section of a YouTube video at any speed from 0.25× to 4×. It is designed
for music practice, language study, choreography, transcription, or any task where the same moment
needs to be watched or heard repeatedly.

## What is included

- A/B points taken from the current video time or entered manually.
- 0.1-second fine adjustment and custom playback speed.
- Pitch preservation always enabled, especially useful for music.
- Keyboard shortcuts for A, B, and starting or stopping the loop.
- A reusable library with names and nested folders.
- Editing, drag-and-drop movement, and duplicate prevention.
- Shareable links carrying the video, A, B, and speed.
- Automatic suspension during YouTube advertisements.
- Spanish and English UI based on the browser language.
- Desktop Firefox, Chrome, and Safari support.

## Your first loop in under a minute

1. [Install YT Looper](#installation) and open a regular YouTube video.
2. Click the extension icon or expand the widget over the video.
3. Play the video and set **A** at the beginning of the fragment.
4. Set **B** at the end.
5. Change the speed if needed.
6. Click **Enable loop**. The video returns to A whenever it reaches B.
7. Use the save icon if you want to keep it in your library.
